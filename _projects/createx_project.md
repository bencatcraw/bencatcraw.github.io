**CreateX Horror Experience** is an experience created using the CreateX lab at UH West Oahu. CreateX is an array of projectors that project videos and applications onto the walls and floors of the room to create immersive experiences. The horror experience was made in Unity as part of a team, where I worked on getting the Unity output projected across the room, the scripted scares and events, and how visitors interact with the room.
