**Life Before Death** is a walking simulator/escape room game focused on interactive storytelling, diverse puzzles, and high quality graphics. It was my first attempt at a larger scale Unreal Engine project, focused on beautiful graphics and level design. I was the lead developer on the team, working on the puzzles and gameplay and helping coordinate the team.
