**Arcane Interns** is a 1-4 player co-op first-person roguelike dungeon crawler, where you play as homunculus interns sent by a wizard into a procedurally shifting dungeon to retrieve loot and complete contracts. Along the way, teams scavenge for gear, customize their weapons with perks and stickers, and upgrade their armor across multiple floors. It is being made in Unity at Studio 300, where I am the lead programmer and also work on 3D animation. Arcane Interns is in active development, with a full release on the way. More information is available **[here](https://studio300games.com/pages/games/arcaneinterns.html)**.
